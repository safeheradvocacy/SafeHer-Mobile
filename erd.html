Figure 4. Entity-Relationship Diagram

Scoped to SafeSpace's 19 core domain entities (geography, identity, case management, shelter services, emergency/law-enforcement, barangay action, communication, support, and system audit). Implementation-level tables that support but do not define the domain — security passcode verifiers, backup/restore jobs, QR access tokens, push-notification tokens, FAQs, and individual chat messages — are intentionally scoped out of this primary diagram. PK marks the primary key; FK marks a foreign key, shown with its referenced entity. Cardinality is labeled 1 (one) / N (many) at each line end.

Figure 17. Level-2 Data Flow Diagram

Decomposes Process 2.0 “Process Incident Reports” from Figure 16 (Level-1 DFD) into its six constituent sub-processes. Every external entity crossing this diagram's boundary — Survivor/Reporter, Barangay Admin, Municipal Admin, Provincial Staff, and Google Maps — and every flow into or out of them is exactly the set Figure 16 shows entering or leaving Process 2.0: nothing is added or dropped at the boundary, only expanded in detail. Provincial Admin is correctly absent here, the same as in Figure 16 — it holds no cases.view permission, so it was never connected to Process 2.0 at any level.

Figure 16 abstracted case handling into two stores, D3 Case Reports and D4 Case History & Referrals. This level reveals the finer-grained tables underneath that abstraction: case assignment and case-service records each get their own store (D12, D13), and D4 here is scoped specifically to the status-history table it was always backed by — the case_referrals half of D4 belongs to Process 5.0 and is out of this diagram's scope.
